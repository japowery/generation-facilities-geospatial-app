Methodology

How the application interprets the source extracts

  • One detailed map marker represents one plant ID, not one generator row; nearby plants aggregate at broad zoom levels.
  • Facility capacity is the sum of generator rows that pass the current filters.
  • “Active” and “retired” reflect the two source extracts; detailed operating statuses remain available in facility drill-downs.
  • Reported retirement dates are shown as provided and are not independently forecast.
